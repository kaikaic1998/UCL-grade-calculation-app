How the grades work · sources & assumptions

All modules count by their credits. With the default structure, final mark = (2 × taught average + project mark) ÷ 3. Component percentages, module marks and the final mean are rounded to two decimal places.

  • Level 7 module pass: 50.00%, plus any configured separate-pass requirements.
  • Distinction: overall ≥69.50%, or ≥68.50% with marks ≥70.00% in at least half of all credits.
  • Merit: overall ≥59.50%, or ≥58.50% with marks ≥60.00% in at least half of all credits.
  • The project must be passed. The taught MSc scheme adds no separate 70% project requirement for Distinction.
  • Standard condonement: up to 30 taught credits at 40.00–49.99%, subject to all award requirements. Set each affected module’s condonement designation before relying on this.
  • Unknown separate-pass designations are assumed to impose no additional threshold for the displayed scenario; results remain conditional. Known must-pass failures block award eligibility.
  • COMP0078’s two group assessments are combined into 14%; split them when you know their individual weights.
  • Editing the total away from 180 credits or using other than one project creates a custom scenario. A 180-credit setup alone does not establish programme compliance.

Official sources: 2026/27 classification, §4.5 · Module assessment, §2 · Award & condonement, §3 · 180-credit programme · Older 120 + 60 credit structure.
